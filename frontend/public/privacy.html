Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy describes how Happy Valley Digital LLC (“Blitzboard,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use Blitzboard Studio and related services.

Information We Handle

Account and sign-in information. When you sign in, we receive information such as your name, email address, email-verification status, and identity identifiers needed to authenticate your account. Sign-in is provided through Microsoft Entra External ID. If the sign-in flow offers Google, Microsoft, Apple, or another identity provider, that provider authenticates you with Entra; Blitzboard does not receive your provider password.

Profile and workspace information. We process profile names, coaching titles, preferences, workspace names and settings, membership roles, and invitation details such as recipient email, role, status, and expiry.

Coaching content. We store information you or your workspace submit, which can include playbooks, plays, scouting reports, game plans, practice plans and scripts, schedules, notes, annotations, and team logos.

Operational information. The service may create diagnostic records such as an error identifier, request method, and operation name to investigate failures and protect service reliability. Authentication tokens and passwords are not intended to be recorded in application logs.

How We Use Information

  • Provide sign-in, account profiles, workspaces, play design, scheduling, exports, and other requested features.
  • Verify identity, enforce workspace roles, and deliver recipient-bound invitations.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the service, and respond to support requests.
  • Meet legal obligations and protect the rights, safety, and integrity of users and the service.

We do not currently use the information in Blitzboard for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information.

How Information Is Shared

Workspace members. Information and coaching content are available to members of the workspace according to their roles. Workspace Owners can manage membership and settings. Only invite people who should have access to that workspace.

Service providers. We use Microsoft identity services for sign-in and Microsoft Azure services for application hosting, data storage, and file storage when the hosted service is enabled. Federated identity providers may process information under their own privacy terms. These providers process information to provide their services to us.

Other disclosures. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer, subject to applicable law.

Blitzboard does not currently initiate subscription payments or automatically send invitation emails. If these capabilities are introduced, we will update this policy to describe the relevant providers and data use.

Browser Storage and Sign-In

Blitzboard uses browser storage for sign-in session state, authentication-library token caching, preferences, and temporary invitation handling. Microsoft Entra and any identity provider may also use cookies or similar technologies on their own pages. You can manage browser storage through your browser settings; clearing it may sign you out or remove local preferences.

Retention and Your Choices

We keep information while it is needed to provide the service, maintain workspace records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Workspace Owners and authorized members can manage or delete some workspace content through available features.

Some workspaces may become read-only and lose in-app access after the configured retention period, currently up to 18 months after a subscription or trial reaches the applicable inactive state. Reaching that limit does not currently trigger automatic deletion of stored content. Account deletion is not self-service.

To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, contact breontechllc@hotmail.com. We may need to verify your identity and coordinate with the workspace Owner. Some information may be retained when required for security, legal, or operational reasons.

Security

We use access controls and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No online service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your sign-in account secure and report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

Children’s Privacy

Blitzboard Studio is a coaching and team-management service, not a service directed to children. Do not submit a child’s personal information unless you have authority to do so and the applicable laws and team policies permit it.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will post the revised version here and update the “Last updated” date. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to breontechllc@hotmail.com.
